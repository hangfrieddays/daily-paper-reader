## 摘要
真实的物理交互是具身智能的基石，但采集成对的视觉-触觉数据仍然成本高昂。视觉到触觉的合成提供了一种有前景的方法来扩充此类数据，然而由于视觉外观与接触相关材料属性之间存在差距，以及成对观测中的空间错位，学习这种映射变得复杂。为应对这些挑战，我们提出 PhysTacGen，一个视觉到光学触觉图像生成框架，它将材料感知描述与几何条件相结合。首先，我们引入组触觉策略优化（Group Tactile Policy Optimization, GTPO），这是一种强化学习策略，利用任务特定奖励来改进视觉-语言模型，以生成结构化的材料描述。其次，我们将基于 DINOv2 的配对筛选与单目相对深度估计相结合，以选择训练配对并提供几何先验。最后，SDXL ControlNet 以 RGB、相对深度和 GTPO 生成的文本为条件，合成光学触觉图像。在整理后的 SSVTP 数据上的实验表明，与对比基线相比，结构相似度有所提高，而一项盲法用户研究显示人们更偏好 GTPO 生成的描述。生成的触觉输入还提升了由属性导出的力系数预测代理任务上的性能。这些结果共同证明了 PhysTacGen 在光学触觉图像合成方面的有效性及其在所评估下游任务中的实用性。代码将发布于 https://github.com/VDIGPKU/PhysTacGen。

## Abstract
Realistic physical interaction is a cornerstone of embodied intelligence, yet collecting paired visual--tactile data remains costly. Visual-to-tactile synthesis offers a promising approach to augmenting such data, but learning this mapping is complicated by the gap between visual appearance and contact-related material properties, as well as spatial misalignment in paired observations. To address these challenges, we present \textbf{PhysTacGen}, a visual-to-optical-tactile image generation framework that integrates material-aware descriptions with geometric conditioning. First, we introduce Group Tactile Policy Optimization (GTPO), a reinforcement learning strategy that refines a vision--language model to generate structured material descriptions using task-specific rewards. Second, we combine DINOv2-based pair curation with monocular relative-depth estimation to select training pairs and provide geometric priors. Finally, an SDXL ControlNet synthesizes optical tactile images conditioned on RGB, relative depth, and GTPO-generated text. Experiments on curated SSVTP data demonstrate improved structural similarity over the compared baselines, while a blinded user study shows a preference for GTPO-generated descriptions. Generated tactile inputs also improve performance on an attribute-derived force-coefficient prediction proxy. Together, these results demonstrate the effectiveness of PhysTacGen for optical tactile image synthesis and its utility in the evaluated downstream task.The code will be available at https://github.com/VDIGPKU/PhysTacGen.
